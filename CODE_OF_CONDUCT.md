# Code of Conduct

EduMind is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community for contributors, learners, maintainers, and users.

Participants should communicate professionally, assume good intent, focus feedback on the work, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private data, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report serious or private concerns to the maintainers through the confidential process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
